# What it understands

Write the way you'd say it out loud. Linewise reads the sentence, works out the maths and keeps the units.

Plain words

Percentages, averages, ratios and rounding, written as sentences.

Every currency, live rates

160+ currencies and crypto. Rates refresh on their own and are kept for when you're offline. Pick ExchangeRate-API, the European Central Bank, Coinbase or Currency API.

Dates, times and time zones

Countdowns, workdays, durations and the time anywhere. The first three answers are live for you right now.

Units, kitchen included

Length, weight, volume, area, temperature, data, speed, fuel economy, power, energy, pressure and CSS pixels. It knows a cup of butter from a cup of flour.

Money and rates

Compound interest, loan repayments, hourly rates and salaries per month.

Names, totals and tags

Name a number and use it below. Refer back with prev, sum, avg or line 3. Tag lines with # and total them.

Yes-or-no checks

Compare across units, and let “if … then … else” pick the number.

Your own functions

Write a formula once and use it anywhere in the sheet. Units flow through it.

Exact to 38 digits

Decimal maths, not floating point, so 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3 and big amounts keep every digit.

Your number format

Answers follow the format you choose in Preferences.

# Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. After the 30-day trial you pay once, $19.99 for you or $39.99 for your family, and Linewise is yours.

What happens when the trial ends?

Linewise asks you to buy it before you carry on. Your sheets are kept exactly as they were, and they're all there the moment you buy.

Does one purchase cover my Mac, iPad and iPhone?

Yes. Linewise is one app with Universal Purchase, so buying it, or starting the trial, on one device covers the others signed in to the same Apple Account.

Where do exchange rates come from?

From the source you choose in Preferences: ExchangeRate-API, the European Central Bank, Coinbase (which adds crypto) or Currency API. Rates refresh on their own and are kept for when you're offline.

How do locked sheets work?

Lock any sheet or folder and it stays hidden until you unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Adding or removing a lock, opening the Bin and deleting anything locked all ask first.

What if Linewise doesn't understand a line?

It leaves it alone as a note. Words it doesn't need are ignored, so you can write freely around your numbers.

Which languages does it speak?

The app is in English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Brazilian Portuguese. You type maths in English words anywhere.
